7. DELETING LOCAL GAME DATA

Users may remove locally stored application data through their device settings.

7.1. Android Devices

  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Select Apps or Applications.
  3. Find and select the relevant KC Game application.
  4. Open Storage or Storage & Cache.
  5. Select Clear Storage or Clear Data, if available.
  6. Alternatively, uninstall the application.

The names of these options may vary depending on the Android version and device manufacturer.

7.2. iOS Devices

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select General.
  3. Select iPhone Storage.
  4. Choose the relevant KC Game application.
  5. Select Delete App.

Deleting an application may remove locally stored gameplay progress and application preferences.

Important: Clearing local data or uninstalling an application does not automatically delete information previously transmitted to third-party service providers.

Users should submit a deletion request if they wish to request deletion of eligible remotely processed personal information.

Anonymous or aggregated information that cannot reasonably identify an individual may not be subject to individual deletion requests.
